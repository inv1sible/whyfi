BLE signature catalog (Fieldwatch)
----------------------------------

backend/scans/signatures/fieldwatch-signatures-v2.json is vendored unmodified
from Fieldwatch (https://github.com/OffGridPete/Fieldwatch), commit
2f79baf95a1a9cdfcc8261f3fb58079aeb75f191, and shipped in the Android app as
android/app/src/main/assets/fieldwatch-signatures-v2.json (byte-identical).
The matching rules in backend/scans/ble_signatures.py and wifi_signatures.py
are a Python port of Fieldwatch's SignatureEngine.kt and related helpers, and
android/app/src/main/java/com/whyfi/app/signatures/ a Kotlin port of those.

The catalog's OUI (MAC address prefix) values and Bluetooth company
identifiers / 16-bit service UUIDs derive from public assigned-number
registries, which are not licensed as MIT by Off Grid Pete LLC:

  IEEE Registration Authority (MA-L / MA-M / MA-S / CID)
  https://standards-oui.ieee.org/

  Bluetooth SIG Assigned Numbers
  https://bitbucket.org/bluetooth-SIG/public/src/main/assigned_numbers/

Use of those values is subject to the IEEE and Bluetooth SIG terms.

The Android app bundles `android/app/src/main/assets/ble_assigned.tsv`, a
conversion of the Bluetooth SIG Assigned Numbers lists (company
identifiers, member UUIDs, service UUIDs) from
https://bitbucket.org/bluetooth-SIG/public/src/main/assigned_numbers/,
used to name BLE advertisers. Subject to the Bluetooth SIG terms.
